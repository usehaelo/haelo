# Font sources

Satoshi: Indian Type Foundry / Fontshare. Source: https://api.fontshare.com/v2/css?f[]=satoshi@400,500,700&display=swap . Distributed under the included Fontshare Free Font License (FFL). Official package: https://api.fontshare.com/v2/fonts/download/satoshi .

Plus Jakarta Sans: Google Fonts / Tokotype. Source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans . Distributed under the included SIL Open Font License.

Fonts are hosted locally and loaded through next/font/local with font-display: swap.

Bricolage Grotesque: Google Fonts / atelier triay. Source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bricolage+Grotesque . Locally hosted variable display face (semibold, optical size 14) for the Figma landing page, distributed under the included SIL Open Font License.
